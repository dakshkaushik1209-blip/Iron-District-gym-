## Brand & Style

The design system embodies an unapologetic, disciplined, industrial strength aesthetic engineered for high-performance athletes, serious lifters, and a focused boutique community. It strips away commercial fitness tropes, cheerful soft gradients, and passive wellness imagery in favor of raw iron, chalk-dusted dark rooms, precision architectural engineering, and laser-focused intent.

The brand's visual identity draws from **Industrial High-Contrast & Utilitarian Precision**:
- **Tone:** Uncompromising, authoritative, elite, technical, disciplined.
- **Atmosphere:** Deep cast-iron shadows, directional low-key spotlighting, heavy architectural lines, and stark monochromatic contrasts punctured by high-voltage blast-furnace orange.
- **Design Metaphors:** Calibrated steel weight plates, knurled bar grips, structural I-beams, blueprint lines, and performance telemetry readouts.

## Colors

The palette is anchored in an uncompromising, dark-mode architecture. The atmosphere is created through stacked charcoal surfaces, razor-thin borders, and a singular, electric industrial orange reserved exclusively for high-priority actions and telemetry tags.

### Core Palette
- **Canvas / Root Background:** `#0A0A0A` (Deep pure black)
- **Primary Accent:** `#FF5A1F` (Blast Orange — high-impact CTAs, active telemetry indicators, key badges)
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Card Background):** `#141414` (Subdued charcoal)
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Elevated / Bento Tier):** `#1A1A1A` (Mid charcoal)
- **Surface Elevation 3 (Hover / Interactive Tier):** `#222222`
- **Border / Structural Grids:** `#262626` (Primary architectural borders) or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` (Subtle interior separators)

### Typography & Data
- **Text Headings / Display:** `#FFFFFF` (Pure stark white)
- **Text Body / Informational:** `#A3A3A3` (Balanced neutral grey for comfortable contrast)
- **Text Muted / Metadata:** `#737373` (Technical annotations, units, deactivated states)
- **Inverse Text (on Primary):** `#000000` (Dense black over `#FF5A1F`)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy creates a sharp contrast between mechanical condensed strength and clinical digital clarity:

1. **Headlines (`Barlow Condensed`):** Display copy must consistently appear in uppercase. Tight letter spacing, heavy weights (700/800), and compressed leading evoke stamped steel, athletic discipline, and architectural signage.
2. **Body Text (`Inter`):** Renders editorial copy and programming guides in crisp, legible, neutral humanist grotesk. Maintain generous line heights (`1.6` to `1.7`) and enforce strict line length constraints (`max-w-prose` / 65 characters) against dark backgrounds to eliminate eye fatigue.
3. **Data & Metadata (`Space Mono`):** Applied to telemetry badges, metric readouts (KG, REPS, WATTS), timestamps, and index numbers to echo industrial equipment serial numbers and performance diagnostics.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts rely on structural grids, visible horizontal and vertical containment lines, and asymmetric split columns.

### Grid & Composition Strategy
- **Base Grid:** 12-column modular grid with 1px hairline separation seams (`#262626`).
- **Asymmetric Splits:** Hero sections and program showcases frequently implement 7:5 or 8:4 column splits, anchoring heavy condensed typography against technical facility telemetry or portrait athletics.
- **Bento Facility Grids:** Facility infrastructure is mapped via rigid multi-cell modular blocks with uniform gutters, varying between square 1x1 data modules and wide 2x1 imagery tiles.
- **Section Rhythm:** Sections receive expansive vertical breathing room (`5rem` to `8rem`), allowing each training discipline and program module to stand with high authority.

## Elevation & Depth

This system rejects ambient colored blurs and drop shadows. Depth is achieved via **Architectural Tonal Tiering** and **Precision Wireframing**:

- **Tier 0 (Root Canvas):** `#0A0A0A` with optional subtle CSS cross-hatch or 1px structural column guide overlays.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Containers):** Solid `#141414` bordered with `1px solid #262626`.
- **Tier 2 (Elevated Cells / Interactive Components):** Solid `#1A1A1A` with `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Active / Accent Elevation:** Solid `#FF5A1F` bounding boxes with zero blur; focus is communicated through high-contrast boundary definition rather than elevation z-axis lift.
- **Glass / Atmospheric Accents:** Limited exclusively to low-opacity backdrop overlays (`rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(8px)`) for sticky telemetry headers and status ribbons.

## Shapes

The geometry of the interface is rigid, precise, and industrial:
- **Maximum Corner Radius:** 2px to 4px across buttons, inputs, and container surfaces (`roundedness: 1`).
- **Strict Constraint:** Pill shapes, circular action containers, and loose organic radii are strictly prohibited.
- **Corners as Joists:** Cards, modal frames, and bento blocks feature clean mechanical joints, occasionally augmented with chamfered or technical corner markings on hero elements.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#FF5A1F`, text `#000000`, uppercase `Barlow Condensed` (weight 700, tracking `0.05em`), 2px radius, padding `0.875rem 1.75rem`. Hover shifts to `#E04B14` without elevation shadow.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, border `1px solid #262626`, text `#FFFFFF`. Hover changes border to `#FF5A1F` and background to `rgba(255, 90, 31, 0.05)`.
- **Ghost Action:** Text `#A3A3A3`, hover text `#FFFFFF` with an inline animated monospace indicator (`->`).

### Program & Facility Cards
- Background `#141414`, border `1px solid #262626`, padding `1.5rem` to `2rem`.
- Header houses monospace index tags (`[01 / HYPERTROPHY]`) in `#737373` paired with uppercase display titles.
- Interactive state: Border shifts to `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)` or primary orange on active selection; images inside cards scale subtly without rounded edge distortion.

### Badges & Metadata Chips
- Height `24px`, background `#1A1A1A`, border `1px solid #262626`, radius `2px`.
- Monospace font (`Space Mono`), uppercase, 11px, text `#A3A3A3`.
- Active telemetry state: Border `#FF5A1F`, text `#FF5A1F`, with an optional 6px solid orange status dot.

### Inputs & Form Fields
- Background `#0F0F0F`, border `1px solid #262626`, 2px radius, padding `0.75rem 1rem`.
- Text `#FFFFFF`, placeholder `#525252`.
- Focus state: Border transitions to `1px solid #FF5A1F` with zero outer shadow ring. Labels use `Inter` uppercase tracking `0.06em` at 12px.

### Checkboxes & Segmented Toggles
- Custom square glyphs (18x18px), 2px radius, background `#141414`, border `1px solid #262626`.
- Checked state: Background `#FF5A1F`, border `#FF5A1F`, inner black tick mark.
- Segmented controllers: Contained within a `#141414` bracket track, active segment is `#262626` with a pure white label.

### Data Readouts & Stats Blocks
- Prominent metric numbers set in `Barlow Condensed` (3rem–4rem), pure white `#FFFFFF`.
- Accompanying unit label (`KG`, `LBS`, `RPM`, `PERCENTILE`) rendered below in `Space Mono` at 12px in `#737373`.
