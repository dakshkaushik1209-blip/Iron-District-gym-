ADMISSIONS FAQ

Operational clarity before stepping onto the training floor.

What is the exact cancellation policy? add
Monthly access requires a clean 30-day notice via your telemetry portal. Quarterly protocols require notice 14 days prior to the auto-renew boundary. No predatory cancellation hurdles, no hidden exit fees.
Are there onboarding or initiation fees? add
Zero initiation fees. We reject commercial gym administration surcharges. Your badge is calibrated during your first 20-minute safety & facility induction without additional charges.
Can I freeze membership for injury or travel? add
Yes. Quarterly and Annual lifters may freeze membership for up to 60 cumulative calendar days per year for verified recovery or work travel. Simply submit a freeze date via the app dashboard.
Can I test the platforms before committing? add
Absolutely. We offer a complimentary 90-minute trial session where you can bring your training log, test our calibrated Eleiko bars, and verify our atmosphere suits your training cadence.
How does coaching integrate with standard access? add
All tiers receive platform floor supervision from certified strength staff. Dedicated block programming, video analysis, and meet prep coaching can be attached as modular bolt-ons at any point.
