FREQUENTLY ENQUIRED // ADMISSIONS & PROTOCOL

Everything you need to know about testing the platform, induction, cancellation policies, and freeze options.

01 // What is the cancellation policy for monthly and quarterly memberships? expand_more
Monthly memberships operate on a continuous 30-day billing cycle. You may cancel at any time with a 14-day notice before your next billing date. Quarterly and Annual memberships are pre-paid commitment blocks with prorated refunds only applicable upon documented medical exception.
02 // Is there an initiation or onboarding registration fee? expand_more
Zero initiation fees or commercial fluff charges. Every member undergoes a mandatory 45-minute biometric safety and platform etiquette induction prior to first solo lift at no additional charge.
03 // Can I pause or freeze my membership for travel or recovery? expand_more
Yes. Quarterly and Annual members can freeze their membership for up to 60 days per calendar year in minimum 14-day increments by submitting a request through the member portal.
04 // Can I try the facility before committing to a membership tier? expand_more
Absolutely. We encourage all prospective athletes to book a complimentary Day 1 Trial Session and platform orientation before selecting a tier.
05 // Are coach-led training programs included in the membership? expand_more
Standard memberships provide full independent platform access and facility amenities. Structured coaching programs and 1-on-1 kinematic sessions can be bundled into any tier with preferred member pricing.
